Hive orchestrates your coding agents — Claude, Codex, Pi — to run work as a folder-as-agent pipeline: brainstorm, plan, execute, review, finalize, and ship the PR. No babysitting terminals; tasks advance in parallel in the background and your only job is to answer questions in a doc. Idea → PR is its flagship coding workflow — and you can author your own for writing, research, triage, anything.
