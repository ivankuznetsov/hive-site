Hive is a durable, local-first workflow engine for AI agents.

Run AI workflows that survive

Hive orchestrates Claude, Codex, Pi, and Grok as durable folder-as-agent pipelines. Rough ideas become reviewed outcomes across pull requests, research, content, audits, and operations. Software delivery is the flagship proof, while built-in, installable, and owner-authored workflows use the same inspectable state and recovery model.
