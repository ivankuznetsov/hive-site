Honeycombs

Reviewed workflows for Hive

Honeycombs are versioned Hive workflows published through a maintained registry. Every listing links to its exact-version package page and designated review, and shows all requested permissions before installation.

Review evidence, not a guarantee

Community or Verified status records review evidence. It is not an endorsement, not a safety guarantee, and not a substitute for inspecting the requested permissions and linked evidence yourself. Tier, lifecycle, and permission risk are independent signals.
